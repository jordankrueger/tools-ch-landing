Board packets accumulate. A scanned signature page, a financial report from your accountant, the meeting agenda, and three exhibits show up as separate PDFs the day before the meeting. The PDF Merge tool combines them in the order you want; the Compress tool then knocks the combined file down to email-friendly size.

<div class="callout"><strong>When to use this:</strong> Assembling a board packet, a grant application bundle, or any multi-document submission where the recipient wants one file, not seven.</div>

## How to do it

1. In **PDF Merge**, drag in each PDF in the order you want them stacked. (You can also drag-reorder after they're loaded.)
2. Click **Merge & download**. You get one combined PDF.
3. If the merged file is over ~10 MB, open the **Compress a PDF** tool, drop the merged file in, and pick "Medium" compression. That's usually enough to bring a 30 MB packet down to 8 MB without scanned pages going fuzzy.
4. Send the compressed PDF to your board chair.

## One thing people miss

If you have a 200-page packet, don't compress it once and call it done. Compress with **Low** first and see if it's small enough; only step up to **Medium** or **High** if you have to. Each level loses a bit more text sharpness on scanned pages, and "the auditor's notes are slightly fuzzy" is a real problem on financial submissions.
